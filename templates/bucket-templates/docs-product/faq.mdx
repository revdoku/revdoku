<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Is there a free plan?">
    Yes. The free plan has one project and three members.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can I use Acme on my phone?">
    Yes. Acme has apps for iOS and Android. They show the same data as the web app.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Where is my data stored?">
    Acme stores your data in the region that you choose when you create the workspace.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I delete my account?">
    Open **Settings**, then **Account**, and select **Delete account**. This cannot be undone.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
