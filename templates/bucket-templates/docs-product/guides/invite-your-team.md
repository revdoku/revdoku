---
title: Invite your team
description: Add people to your workspace and choose what they can do.
---

Only an owner or an administrator can invite people.

1. Open **Settings**, then **Members**.
2. Select **Invite**.
3. Enter the email address of the person.
4. Choose a role, then select **Send invitation**.

The person gets an email with a link. The link works for 7 days.

## Roles

| Role | Can do |
| --- | --- |
| Viewer | Read projects |
| Editor | Read and change projects |
| Administrator | Manage projects, members and settings |

## Remove a person

1. Open **Settings**, then **Members**.
2. Select the person, then select **Remove**.

The person loses access at once. Their work stays in the workspace.
