---
title: Getting started
description: Set up Acme in five minutes.
---

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Sign up with your work email. You get a workspace for your team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your first project">
    Select **New project**, enter a name, then select **Create**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your team">
    Open **Settings**, then **Members**, and add the people who work with you. See [Invite your team](/guides/invite-your-team).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Requirements

| Item | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Browser | A current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari |
| Mobile app | iOS 17 or Android 13, or later |
| Desktop app | macOS 13 or Windows 11, or later |
