# Revdoku pricing

Start on the Try free plan, without a card. Paid plans start at $4.99/mo. Prices are in USD.
One agent? Starter. A team of agents? Standard. Apps or clients? Business.

| Plan | Try free | Starter | Standard | Business |

| Founding offer price | — | $24.95/yr | $9.99/mo or $99.95/yr | $24.99/mo or $249.95/yr |
| Incoming emails / month | 200 (128 MiB) | 2,000 (512 MiB) | 5,000 (2 GiB) | 15,000 (5 GiB) |

| Support | Community | Email support | Email support | Email support |

API, MCP and CLI are included on every plan. Your first paid
subscription has a 30-day money-back guarantee on its first payment.

The founding offer takes 50% off for as long as the subscription stays active.
It is for the first 100 customers, once per customer. Checkout applies it to
Starter Annual, Standard and Business, monthly or annual. When it ends, the
pricing page stops showing it.

Every plan can send to account members and the account's other mailboxes. Paid
plans also reply to people who emailed the mailbox.

A mailbox address on `revdokumail.com` is the name you choose plus a 4-character
random suffix, such as `sales.agent.x7k2@revdokumail.com`. On paid plans you can
use your own domain for an exact name, change an address later and add aliases.

The [pricing page](https://app.revdoku.com/pricing) is the source of truth for
current plans and every limit.
