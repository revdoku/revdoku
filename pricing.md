# Revdoku pricing

You can start free, with no card. Prices are in USD.

| Plan | Free | DEV | DEV PRO |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Price | $0 | $4.99/mo | $49.99/mo |
| Incoming emails / month | 3,000 (128 MiB) | 3,000 (1 GiB) | 15,000 (5 GiB) |
| Sent emails / month (replies) | 300 | 3,000 | 15,000 |
| Mailboxes | 3 | 10 | 125 |
| Storage | 1 GB | 10 GB | 50 GB |
| Maximum email size | 10 MiB | 40 MiB | 40 MiB |
| Custom email domains | — | 1 per account | 1 per account |
| AI connections | 3 | 10 | 50 |
| API keys | 1 | 3 | 15 |
| Support | Community | Fanatical support | Fanatical support |

Sent emails are replies to people who emailed the mailbox. On Free, a chosen
address gets a permanent 12-character random suffix after a dot-separated
prefix; an exact address needs a paid plan.

The [pricing page](https://app.revdoku.com/pricing) is the source of truth for
current plans and every limit.
