# Revdoku pricing

Canonical browser page: https://app.revdoku.com/pricing

Prices are USD. New accounts start on Free. No credit card is required.

Anonymous previews are public-only and never create a private bucket. They do not include forms, analytics, ZIP uploads, custom domains, or a custom URL.

Free and anonymous websites use a randomized `<word>-<word>-<4 digits>.revdoku.site` URL. Choosing or renaming the Website Name requires Personal or Developer.

Storage is shared by current files and retained file versions. Revdoku does not impose a fixed visitor or transfer quota on these plans; fair-use and abuse controls apply.
