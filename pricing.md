# Revdoku pricing

Start on the Free plan, without a card. Paid plans start at $4.99/mo. Prices are in USD.
Your own domain? Starter. A team of agents? Standard. Apps or clients? Business.

| Limited-time offer price | — | $24.95/yr | $9.99/mo or $99.95/yr | $24.99/mo or $249.95/yr |
| Incoming emails / month | 3,000 (768 MB) | 5,000 (1 GB) | 10,000 (3 GB) | 30,000 (8 GB) |

| Support | Community | Email support | Email support | Email support |

API, MCP and CLI are included on every plan. Your first paid
subscription has a 30-day money-back guarantee on its first payment.

The limited-time offer takes 50% off for as long as the subscription stays
active, once per customer. Checkout applies it to Starter Annual, Standard and
Business, monthly or annual. When it ends, the pricing page stops showing it.

Every plan can send to account members, the account's other mailboxes and
people who emailed the mailbox. On the Free plan, each sent email ends with a signature line, after the standard `-- ` separator:
"Sent with Revdoku, mailboxes for AI agents: https://revdoku.com".

A mailbox address on `revdokumail.com` is the name you choose plus a 4-character
random suffix, such as `sales.agent.x7k2@revdokumail.com`. On paid plans you can
use your own domain for an exact name, change an address later and add aliases.

The [pricing page](https://app.revdoku.com/pricing) is the source of truth for
current plans and every limit.
