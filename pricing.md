# Revdoku pricing

Start on the Free plan, without a card. Paid plans start at $12/yr. Prices are in USD.
Publish uploaded files or use your own email domain? Starter. Password-protected docs or several products? Standard. Docs for clients? Business.

Starter is billed once a year only. API, MCP and CLI are included on every plan.
Your first paid subscription has a 30-day money-back guarantee on its first payment.

On the Free plan, sites publish from a connected GitHub repository, and each page
shows a small Revdoku badge. On paid plans you can also publish uploaded files and turn the badge off.

Credits pay for messages and AI work: 1 per chat message (also a reply that the visitor reads in the chat),
2 per new chat, 2 per received email, 2 per recipient of a sent email and 10 per AI check of an email.
Ask AI and AI agents use credits by usage. Without credits, chat messages and email still arrive,
but AI and sending pause.

Every plan can send to account members, the account's other mailboxes and
people who emailed the mailbox. On the Free plan, each sent email ends with a signature line, after the standard `-- ` separator:
"Sent with Revdoku: https://revdoku.com".

A mailbox address on `revdokumail.com` is the bucket's name, with no suffix, such as
`acme-docs@revdokumail.com`. The bucket's site uses the same name. On paid plans you can
use your own domain, change an address later and add aliases.

The [pricing page](https://app.revdoku.com/pricing) is the source of truth for
current plans and every limit.
