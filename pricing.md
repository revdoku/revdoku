# Revdoku pricing

You can start free, with no card. Prices are in USD.
One agent? Starter. A team of agents? Standard. Apps or clients? Business.

| Plan | Free | Starter | Standard | Business |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Price | $0 | $4.99/mo | $9.99/mo | $49.99/mo |
| Annual price (2 months free) | — | $49.90/yr | $99.90/yr | $499.90/yr |
| Incoming emails / month | 200 (128 MiB) | 2,000 (512 MiB) | 5,000 (2 GiB) | 15,000 (5 GiB) |
| Sent emails / month | 200 | 500 | 5,000 | 15,000 |
| Mailboxes | 1 | 1 | 10 | 125 |
| Storage | 1 GB | 5 GB | 10 GB | 50 GB |
| Maximum email size | 10 MiB | 40 MiB | 40 MiB | 40 MiB |
| Custom email domains | — | 1 | 1 | 1 per account |
| AI connections | 3 | 5 | 10 | 50 |
| API keys | 1 | 2 | 3 | 15 |
| AI labels and junk filtering | Included | Included | Included | Included |
| Support | Community | Fanatical support | Fanatical support | Fanatical support |

API, MCP and CLI are included on every plan, including Free. Your first paid
subscription has a 30-day money-back guarantee on its first payment.

Every plan, including Free, can reply to people who emailed the mailbox, and
send to account members and the account's other mailboxes.

On Free, a mailbox address is the name you choose plus a 4-character random
suffix, such as `sales.agent.x7k2@revdokumail.com`. On paid plans you choose an
exact name of at least 6 characters, and you can change an address later and add
aliases.

The [pricing page](https://app.revdoku.com/pricing) is the source of truth for
current plans and every limit.
